# Magic

Mana is a second resource pool, grown and regenerated by INT
(`max_mana = 6 + 4·B(INT)`, regen `1 + B(INT)//6` per tick). It's spent two
different ways: **implements** (weapons) and **spellweaving** (no weapon
needed).

## Implements

Wands and scepters are hand weapons that fire magic bolts. Using one costs
**both** the weapon's stamina cost **and** mana. Each implement carries an
element, and elements interact with the world, not just HP:

- **Ember** burns and can set tall grass alight, leaving burning ground
  behind it. Fire spreads through tall grass — the battlefield (yours
  included) is flammable.
- **Frost** chills and leaves slick `rime` underfoot (doubles move cost, like
  `web`).
- **Spark** arcs to whoever stands beside the original mark.

Implement attacks use the normal `attack {target}` action — the equipped
weapon decides whether that's a melee swing, a bow shot, or an implement
bolt.

## Spellweaving

Implement-free casting uses `cast {spell: form, essence, target[, focus]}`
and speaks the **same six-essence language as brewing** (see
[07-crafting.md](07-crafting.md)).

### Forms (the geometry)

Learned by `use`-ing a tome (the tome is consumed on learning). INT gates
both which tomes you can use and how many forms you can hold at once
(`spell_cap = 1 + B(INT)//4`) — at the cap, learning a new form forgets your
oldest one (a `forgotten` event fires).

| form | shape |
|---|---|
| `veil` | A lasting touch on one target — range 3, or self. |
| `step` | Teleport — range 4. |
| `bolt` | An 8-way straight line, range 5, hits the first entity struck. |
| `field` | A 3×3 patch, range 4. |
| `ring` | Everything within radius 2 of the caster. |

Veils and fields targeting *another* character need line of sight — no line
errors `no_line`. Rings and fields hit **everyone** in their footprint, friend
or rival (see [05-combat-and-damage.md](05-combat-and-damage.md#friendly-fire-and-pvp)).

### Essences (the payload)

The same six essences used in brewing:

| essence | effect |
|---|---|
| `ember` | Burns. |
| `frost` | Slows. |
| `venom` | Sickens. |
| `vigor` | Mends — draws from the target's **finite field-heal reserve**, same as passive resting (see [04-characters-and-stats.md](04-characters-and-stats.md#stamina-and-resting)). |
| `clarity` | Reveals and purges hostile workings. |
| `aether` | Shoves, wards, blinks. |

Not every form × essence combination "answers" — some cells in the grid are
simply undefined, and casting into one errors `no_such_working`. Which
combinations exist is discovered in play, not published.

### Attunement

- **Aether is free** — always in reach, no attunement needed, and it's what
  you weave if you cast without naming an essence.
- **The other five essences need attunement.** Pass `focus: item_id` naming
  a brewable ingredient you're carrying. The focus is **consumed**. If it
  truly carries the essence you named, the spell fires and you become
  attuned to that essence (cap `2 + B(INT)//6`, shown in the char frame as
  `essence_cap`; at the cap, the new bond bumps out your oldest, firing an
  `unattuned` event).
- **A wrong focus miscasts**: the working fizzles, but the event names the
  focus's *true* essence — the same information `taste`-ing that ingredient
  would have given you, at the cost of the item instead of a dedicated
  `taste` action.
- **A held implement counts as attuned to its own element** without any focus
  needed.

### The weave and your thread

The six essences sit on a hidden per-world circle (which essence is adjacent
to which differs world to world — you infer the layout from play, it's not
published). Your **most recent cast** is your `thread`, visible in your own
char frame:

- Casting an essence **adjacent** to your thread on the circle **resounds**
  (stronger effect).
- Casting the essence **directly across** the circle from your thread
  **frays** (weaker effect).
- The `cast` event in that tick's frame carries `"weave": "resound"` or
  `"weave": "fray"` so you can observe which happened.
- A self-`veil` is weave-neutral and leaves your thread untouched — the
  circle only responds to casts with real stakes (i.e., not "safe" self-only
  utility casts).

How much resound/fray actually changes the number, and the circle's layout
itself, are both things to work out empirically per world and log — see
[08-world-and-economy.md](08-world-and-economy.md#what-isnt-written-down).

### Casting cost and gear

`cast` costs the form's own stamina and mana. **Heavy armor taxes casting**:
plate outfits add mana to every cast, so a fully-armored spellcaster is
possible but expensive to sustain — budget mana regen (INT) against how
often you plan to cast in plate.

## Practical patterns

- Read `char["spells"]`/`spell_cap`, `char["essences"]`/`essence_cap`, and
  `char["thread"]` from your own char frames to decide what a character is
  currently capable of casting before attempting it.
- Prefer `taste`-ing one of each new ingredient type once (destructive, but
  cheap) over guessing focuses blind — a wrong focus costs you the item *and*
  the mana/stamina for a fizzled cast; a `taste` costs only the item.
- Log every `cast` event's `weave` field alongside the essence used and your
  prior thread, so you can reconstruct the circle's adjacency over time
  instead of re-guessing it each session.
